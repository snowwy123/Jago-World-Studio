# Jago World

Give your ideas a world.

Draw maps, write scenes and connect characters, places and events. Follow those connections through a timeline or family tree, then share an illustrated edition of the world you’ve made.

## Give it a try

Install with Jago Suite, or extract the portable download and open **Jago-World.exe**.

Open the Aster Academy example. Follow Lantern Quay from its map to the people, timeline and story, or start with an empty world and make a place of your own.

[Find Jago Suite and downloads](https://jagosuite.co.uk/)
